how it works

simple enough to actually do.

1

you join feelslikeee club.

one click. that's genuinely the hardest part. everything else just shows up.

2

every Sunday, a challenge lands in your inbox.

named. real. with a story behind it. something you can do this week, whether you have 10 minutes or a whole afternoon.

3

you do it. your way.

no right answer. no performance. no one's grading you. just you, doing the thing, making the memory.

4

you share it. the community shows up.

see how others completed the same challenge. different cities, different lives, same Sunday. that's the bit that makes this feel real.

5

by December, you have 52 stories.

not 52 plans. not 52 saved posts. 52 things you actually did. a year that felt genuinely different.
